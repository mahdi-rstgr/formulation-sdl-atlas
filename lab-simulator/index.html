    Event log

      A simulated campaign. Each experiment makes a premix at thickener fraction x and measures a desirability y (viscosity on target, no separation) from a hidden response curve with noise. Optimizers fit a Gaussian process and pick the next x by upper confidence bound; agents plan and draft recoveries but act only through the orchestrator, behind a human-approval gate.
